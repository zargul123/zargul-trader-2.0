       * Head 2 (Signal Classifier): Predicts the action (buy, sell, or hold). This head is failing. It's still too hesitant and defaults to

   2. The Contradiction: The two heads are contradicting each other. The price-prediction head says "Big move coming!" while the
      classification head says "I'm not sure, let's hold."

   3. Why is this happening? Even with the corrected training labels, the "hold" category is still naturally the largest. It's harder for the
      classification head to gain high confidence on the less frequent "buy" and "sell" signals. It has learned to be cautious, while the
      price-prediction head is free to predict the magnitude of the move without that same classification constraint.

  The Solution: A Logical Override

  The fix is to make the two heads agree. We will add a logical override in the predict function. We will trust the head that is giving us
  the strong, actionable data (the pct_change) and use it to correct the hesitant head.

   1. Location: The change will be in scripts/core/analysis_engine.py, inside the predict method.
   2. The Logic: After the model makes its initial prediction, we will add a new rule:
       * IF the predicted direction is "hold"...
       * AND the absolute pct_change is significant (e.g., greater than 0.5%)...
       * THEN we will override the "hold" signal and set the direction based on the pct_change:
           * If pct_change is positive, the new direction will be "long".
           * If pct_change is negative, the new direction will be "short".

  This will force the AI to act on its own high-conviction price predictions, directly resolving the issue you're seeing and allowing the
